2. What Rhumi is

Rhumi gives you one verse of the Qur'an each day, with a short reflection written in plain modern English, chosen in part by how you tell us you're feeling. It also lets you keep a private journal and ask questions about the Qur'an and hadith.

Rhumi's reflections are our own editorial writing. They are a companion to the verse — not scholarship, not tafsir, and not religious rulings. Verses are quoted from a published translation and are never written or altered by us.

Treat its answers as a starting point for your own reading, not as an authority. Every answer carries a note saying so. If an answer is wrong or troubling, please report it using the button on the answer, or email us.
